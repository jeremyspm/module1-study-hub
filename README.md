# Module 1 Study Hub — Health Science 2 (722.541)

A single-file, offline study hub for **Module 1 — Cardiovascular, Respiratory & Lymphatic**, built as a **pre-class deep dive** for the Bachelor of Nursing at Manukau Institute of Technology (NZ).

**▶ Open the hub:** https://jeremyspm.github.io/module1-study-hub/

It works entirely in the browser with no login, no install and no internet needed once loaded — your progress and any images you pin are saved on your own device.

## What's inside

- **Start Here** — a plain-English orientation to the three systems, written to make sense straight after Health Science 1.
- **Six topics** (Blood Vessels · Heart & Blood Flow · Cardiac Physiology · Blood Pressure · Respiratory · Lymphatic), each with:
  - **Orient me first** — a cold-start primer, key terms, optional videos, and why it matters for nursing.
  - **See it** — clean anatomy diagrams embedded directly, plus links to the matching free OpenStax textbook sections.
  - **The questions** — the module's self-test questions with worked model answers (the backbone of the hub).

## How to use it

1. Read **Orient me first** for the big picture.
2. Look at **See it** and study the diagrams.
3. Work through **The questions** — cover the answer, say your version out loud, then reveal.

## Disclaimer

This is a **student-made study aid**, not official course material. The model answers are written to help you learn and revise, and should be checked against your lecturer's teaching and your set texts (Patton & Thibodeau; Watson; Craft et al.; Hampton). Always defer to your course for assessment.
